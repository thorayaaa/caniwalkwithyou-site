## Part 1: Community Guidelines

CIWWY is for walking and talking: real people, real voices, real feelings. You'll hear about heartbreak, grief, family and the decisions that keep people up at night. These rules keep it safe to be that honest. They apply to everything on CIWWY: walks, answers, photos, routes, profiles and names.

**You can talk about hard things here. Just keep it gentle.** Talk about what happened and how it felt: nothing violent or sexual described in detail, no details of how someone hurt themselves, and never make harm sound like the answer. Someone walking with you might be struggling. Leave out anything that could identify someone. Change names if you need to.

You must be 18 or older to use CIWWY.

### 1. Safety and kindness

Talk about anything hard. Don't hurt anyone while you do it.

**Not allowed:**
- Hate speech or racism: attacking or putting people down because of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, immigration status or serious illness. Slurs, "jokes" and stereotypes count.
- Harassment and bullying: insulting, shaming, mocking or ganging up on someone, including in private answers.
- Threats of violence, even as a "joke", and urging others to hurt someone.
- Praising, promoting or recruiting for violent extremist or hate groups.
- Using "pass the mic" answers to keep contacting someone who doesn't want to hear from you.

**Allowed:** Talking about racism, abuse or violence you lived through, and about someone who hurt you, as long as you don't name or expose them (see Privacy).

### 2. Sexual content and nudity

CIWWY has no sexual content.
- No sexually explicit talk, no descriptions of sex acts, and no audio meant to arouse.
- No nudity or sexual images in cover, spot or profile photos.
- No sexual messages or requests in answers, and no asking people to move to another app for sexual reasons.
- Never share intimate images or recordings of anyone without their consent.

**Allowed:** Talking honestly about relationships, dating and intimacy in general, non-graphic terms.

### 3. Keeping minors safe

- No content that sexualizes, grooms or endangers anyone under 18. We report child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and permanently ban the account.
- Don't record children, or post photos where children can be identified.
- Don't try to contact or meet minors through CIWWY.

### 4. Self-harm, suicide and eating disorders

Hard topics are welcome here: grief, illness, abuse, assault, violence, self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, addiction. You can talk about pain here. Many people walk because they're struggling, and hearing someone else's story can help. Say what happened and how it felt; graphic details aren't allowed, because someone walking with you might be struggling.

**Allowed (answer "yes" to "did you talk about anything sensitive?"):**
- Your own experience with suicidal thoughts, self-harm, an eating disorder, or losing someone to suicide.
- Saying that something happened to you, like an assault or abuse, and how it felt, without describing it.
- Recovery stories, coping, and what helped you.
- Encouraging someone to get support.

**Not allowed (the check holds that part; you can cut it, ask a person to check it, or keep it just for you):**
- Graphic violence or injury described in detail (how someone was stabbed, gore).
- Details of how someone hurt themselves or could (methods, amounts, places).
- Anything that makes suicide, self-harm or disordered eating sound appealing or encourages it, including tips like calorie targets or hiding symptoms, extreme weight-loss challenges, or body-shaming.
- Sexual detail, including detailed descriptions of sexual abuse (saying it happened is fine; describing it is not).
- Detailed descriptions of abuse or cruelty to people or animals.
- Identifying or targeting a private person: a first and last name together with something negative, accusing, sexual or private about them; their home address, workplace, school, phone number, email, socials or license plate; or asking anyone to find, contact, expose, harass or hurt them. Saying a name is fine: a first name, the person you're walking with, "my mom", made-up names, public figures.
- Threats, hate, or urging anyone to hurt someone, including telling someone they should hurt themselves.

Anything sexual involving minors is removed and reported (see section 3), never held for cutting or review.

**Need to talk to someone now?** In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). Outside the US, find your country's free helpline at findahelpline.com. If you're in danger right now, call your local emergency number (911 in the US). Can I Walk With You? isn't monitored in real time and isn't an emergency service. If you're in danger, call or text 988 in the US, or your local emergency number.

### 5. Dangerous acts and walking safety

- Watch where you're going. Don't let recording pull your attention from traffic, trains, cliffs, water or crowds.
- Don't trespass, and don't encourage others to go onto private property, closed areas, rail tracks, rooftops or other unsafe places.
- No dangerous challenges, stunts or dares that could get someone hurt.
- Don't walk while intoxicated in an unsafe way, and don't encourage it.

### 6. Regulated goods and illegal activity

- Don't sell, trade or promote drugs, weapons, alcohol, tobacco, vapes or gambling.
- Don't help anyone commit a crime, including theft, scams, fraud, hacking and human trafficking.
- No ads or selling of any kind.

**Allowed:** Talking about your own past with addiction or recovery, or legal trouble you've been through.

### 7. Privacy

- **People in your story:** don't name or identify private people (an ex, a boss, a family member) in a way that exposes them. "My ex" is fine. Their full name with something negative or private about them, their workplace or their socials are not. Leave out details that could identify someone. Change names if you need to.
- **No personal info:** don't share anyone's address, phone number, email, workplace, school, license plate, medical info or private messages. That includes your own home address.
- **Photos of others:** don't post photos where other people's faces can be identified unless they agreed. Crowds in the background are fine if no one is the focus.
- **Bystanders:** don't record other people's private conversations, phone calls or arguments, even on a public street. California law requires everyone's consent to record a confidential conversation.
- **Answers are personal:** don't screen-record, copy or repost someone's walk or answer outside CIWWY without their permission.
- **Anonymous posts:** don't try to find out who posted something anonymously.
- **Routes:** we hide the start and end of your route, but avoid starting or ending a walk right at your home anyway.

### 8. Be real

- Don't pretend to be another person, brand or organization.
- No spam, which includes repeated posts, mass answers, fake accounts and bots.
- Don't share false claims that could cause real harm, like dangerous health advice, scams or fake emergencies.
- Don't use AI or edited voices to make it sound like a real person said something they didn't. CIWWY's own "change your voice" option is fine.
- Don't make new accounts to get around a ban.

### 9. Music and other people's work

- Don't play music, podcasts, movies, audiobooks or other copyrighted audio in your walk or answer, including music from a café speaker. Turn it off or move away.
- Only post photos you took or have the right to use.
- If you think something on CIWWY uses your work without permission, email us.

### 10. What happens when rules are broken

Every walk and answer is checked before anyone hears it. An AI reads a written transcript of the audio, and reports can hide a walk by themselves. Nobody listens unless the check holds something or someone reports it. Depending on how serious it is, we may:
- **Hold** a walk or answer and ask you to edit or re-record it.
- **Add a sensitive-topics heads-up** if you didn't.
- **Remove** a walk, answer, photo or route.
- **Limit** features, like answering or posting publicly, for a while.
- **Suspend** your account temporarily.
- **Ban** your account permanently. Severe violations (threats, child exploitation, sharing intimate images, exposing someone's personal info) can mean a ban the first time.

**Report and block:** tap report on any walk, answer or profile. Tap block to stop someone from hearing your walks or sending you answers.

**Think we got it wrong?** Removed walks can't be restored. Email hello@caniwalkwithyou.com within 30 days with your username and what was removed. A person, not the AI, may look at it again. If we got it wrong, it won't count against your account.
