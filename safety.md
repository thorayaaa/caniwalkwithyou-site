# safety tips

<p class="updated">last updated {{ site.last_updated }}</p>

routes on can i walk with you? are made by other walkers. we don't visit, check or vouch for them, and places change. you decide whether a route is right for you, every time.

**in an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.** the app isn't an emergency service.

## walking a route

- **go in daylight** when you can, especially on a route you don't know.
- **stick to well-traveled places,** and areas you already know.
- **tell someone where you're going,** and when you expect to be back.
- **watch where you're going, not your phone.** stop before you read a prompt or take a photo, and follow traffic rules.
- **respect private property** and the rules of every place you visit. if a stop is somewhere you shouldn't go, skip it.
- **trust your gut.** if anything feels wrong, stop or turn back - that matters more than finishing the route.
- **walk within your own limits,** and check with a doctor if you're not sure walking is right for you.

## making a route

- **use public places** that are legal and reasonably safe to walk - for the start and every stop.
- **never private property** without permission, and **never someone's home** - not even your own. the app warns you if a start looks like a home address.
- **watch where you're going while you make it,** not your phone.
- **think about who'll walk it next:** a stranger, maybe after dark, maybe in the rain. if a stretch only feels okay because you know it, leave it out.
- **keep prompts safe.** ask walkers to notice, find or photograph something - never to do anything risky.

## something unsafe?

if you or someone else is in danger, call 911 or your local emergency number first. then report it in the app (tap "⋯"), or tell us at <a href="mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}">{{ site.contact_email }}</a>.
