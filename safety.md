# safety tips

<p class="updated">last updated {{ site.last_updated }}</p>

can i walk with you? is talk-walks: you hear someone's voice while you walk, or talk while you walk for someone to hear later. the walk is yours - where you go, how far and when you stop is always up to you.

**in an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.** the app isn't an emergency service, and nobody watches walks as they happen.

## walking with someone's voice

- **watch where you're going, not your phone.** stop before you look at the screen, and follow traffic rules.
- **one earbud in is safest,** and keep the volume low enough to hear cars, bikes and people around you.
- **you never have to do what a talker says.** skip anything that doesn't feel right - a turn, a question, a place.
- **stop whenever you like.** sit down, take a break, or end the walk - answers sent to you can wait, and "on a break" or "can't walk?" lets you keep listening without walking.
- **go in daylight** when you can, and stick to places you know or that feel busy and well lit.
- **tell someone where you're going** if you're walking somewhere new.
- **walk within your own limits,** and check with a doctor if you're not sure walking is right for you.

## talking while you walk

- **watch where you're going while you talk.** traffic, trains, cliffs, water and crowds come first; the recording can wait.
- **don't say where you live,** or anything that would let a stranger find you or someone else. the start and end of your route are always hidden, but your words aren't.
- **don't record other people** who haven't agreed to it, and don't name people who could be hurt by it.
- **never ask listeners to do anything risky.**

## something unsafe?

if you or someone else is in danger, call 911 or your local emergency number first. if you need to talk to someone now, call or text 988 in the US, or find your country's line at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).
