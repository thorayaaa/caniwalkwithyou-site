# privacy policy

<p class="updated">last updated November 1, 2026</p>

<div class="short" markdown="1">
**the short version**

- can i walk with you? records voices. talkers record their walks. a listener's microphone is on only during their turn to answer, and they're told first.
- you choose who hears what you recorded: just you, the talker, or everyone - under your name or anonymously, in your own voice or a changed one.
- before anything is shared, and only if you say ok, your phone turns it into text, and an AI service (Anthropic) checks only that text - never the recording. if your phone can't turn it into text, or a recording is reported, a speech-to-text service (Deepgram) turns it into text for that check. neither uses it to train AI.
- we never identify anyone by their voice. no voiceprints.
- we don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
- you can delete a walk, an answer, or your whole account at any time in the app, and it's deleted from the app right away.
- questions: hello@caniwalkwithyou.com

this policy covers the app and this website.

## what we collect, and why

### your account

- **your email address**, so you can sign in, confirm your email with a 6-digit code, reset a forgotten password and change your email. if you sign in with Apple, we get the email Apple gives us (which can be a private relay address). Apple may also offer your name; we don't keep it.
- **your password** is handled by our database provider (Supabase) and stored scrambled (hashed). we never see it.
- **your handle** (like @walker) and, if you add them, a **display name**, **bio**, **profile photo**, **Instagram username** and **the city you set**.
- **your age: only "18 or older: yes or no"**, and when it was checked. the app asks for your date of birth only to check this. the date itself is never kept - not on your phone, not on our server. if the answer is "no", everything that account posted is hidden from everyone.
- **the agreements you tick**, so there's a record: the sign-up box (the terms, this policy, and that you're 18 or older), the community rules before your first walk, and agreeing to updated terms - which box, its exact words and the date.
- **your choices in settings**: private profile, "walking isn't possible for me" (we don't ask why), "hide walks with sensitive topics", "remind me if i talk while paused", your "share in other media" default, your notification choices, and your answer to the AI check question below. only you can see them.

### your recordings

- **talkers:** while you record a talk-walk, the app records **your voice**, **the path you walk** (see "your location"), and the moments you mark a spot or pass the mic. the recording stays on your phone until you post it or save it for later (both upload it). parts you cut while re-recording are deleted. the moments you mark a spot or pass the mic (their times, not places) are also saved to your account as you go, so a lost or closed phone doesn't lose them; only you can see them, and they're deleted 2 days later.
- **while your walk is paused** the microphone stays on, so it carries on the moment you tap talk (the app and the lock screen say "paused · mic on so you can resume."). **nothing it hears while paused is recorded, saved or uploaded.** your phone listens only for you saying "resume" (with Apple's speech recognition on your phone, if you allowed it) and, if "remind me if i talk while paused" is on, checks how loud it is so it can say "you're paused. tap talk to keep recording." if you talk for a few seconds. none of this leaves your phone. after 10 minutes paused the microphone turns fully off ("paused for 10 minutes, so your mic is off. unlock to continue.").
- **listeners:** while you walk with someone, your microphone is **off while they talk** - nothing is recorded then. it turns on only when they pass you the mic, and records **your answer** until you're done (or skip, which deletes that take, or redo, which records it again). a turn is up to 5 minutes; after 30 seconds of silence the app asks "still there?", and after a minute of silence the turn ends. at the end of the walk you can say anything back, for up to 20 minutes. the microphone turns off the moment a turn ends. the app tells you this before you start.
- **when your walk with someone ends**, you choose what happens to your answers: keep them for yourself (saved to your account, only you can hear them), send them to the talker, share them publicly, or save them for later. your answers are uploaded as one recording, with the place of each answer in the walk.
- **your journal:** if you walk your own walk ("walk it yourself"), what you say is saved as a dated journal entry that only you can hear. it's never sent, shared or checked.
- **voice commands:** during your turn you can say "skip", "redo" or "i'm done". your phone listens for these words itself, with Apple's speech recognition **on your phone**; nothing is sent anywhere for it.
- **changed voices** are made on your phone. when you post or send with a changed voice, only the changed version is uploaded. **one exception:** an answer you **save for later** is uploaded in your own voice; if you later pick a changed voice for it, the changed version replaces it. your loudness is evened out on your phone before upload.
- **what's saved with a walk:** its recording, name, description, topics, the spots you marked (their names, places, and the photos or videos you chose), the moments you passed the mic, its length, distance and moving time, whether your phone says you walked (see "motion"), the city and country it started in, its cover photo, the path you walked, and your choices (who can listen, anonymous, your voice, your route, how much of the start and end to hide, "share in other media").
- **what's saved with an answer:** the recording of your turns, where each one was in the walk, your note if you write one, photos you add ("your view"), the city you were in (a city name only), how far you moved and for how long (numbers only, for the walking rule), whether your phone says you walked (see "motion"), your route - only if you choose "show", and without its start and end - and your choices.
- **photos and videos:** only the ones you pick are uploaded. photos are re-saved without their hidden details (like where they were taken), and a video's location is removed before upload.
- **on your phone:** after you post a walk, its recording (and its changed-voice copy and preview) stays in the app's own storage on your phone. an answer's recording is removed from your phone once it's sent. walks you listen to are downloaded to play, and removed from your phone 2 hours after you finish (or 24 hours after you last played one you paused). deleting the app removes all of it, and so does settings › about › tap the version number 5 times › "clear leftover recordings on this phone" (which never touches what's on our server). your iPhone's own backups can include the app's storage until your next backup.

### the AI check

when you post a walk for everyone, or send or share an answer, it's checked before anyone else can hear it.

- **we ask you first.** when you post a walk for everyone or share an answer, the app asks whether it can be checked. **nothing - no text and no recording - is sent to the AI check or to Deepgram unless you say ok.** if you say no, your walk or answer is saved just for you, and nobody else can see or hear it until you turn on the safety check (in settings › the safety check, or when the app asks you next time); then it's checked, and posted if it passes. we keep your answer and when you gave it.
- **your phone turns the recording into text** with a time for each word, using Apple's speech recognition **on your phone** - the recording isn't sent anywhere for this. iOS asks you once to allow speech recognition. if your phone can't make the text, or you've turned speech recognition off, a speech-to-text company, Deepgram, turns the recording into text on our server instead (see the next point), and the AI checks that text before anyone else can hear it.
- **what Anthropic gets:** the text with its word times, and the walk's name, description, spot names and topics (for an answer: your note). **not** your recording, name, handle, email, account id, city or location. Anthropic (Claude) checks it against our community guidelines. under its commercial terms it **doesn't use it to train its models**, and deletes it within 30 days - except that it can keep content its own safety systems flag as breaking its usage policy for up to 2 years.
- **if your phone can't make the text, or someone reports a walk or an answer,** a speech-to-text company, Deepgram, turns its recording into text so the AI check can read what was really said. this happens only in those two cases, and only if the person who made it said ok to the AI check (if they didn't, nothing is sent to Deepgram, and their walks and answers stay just for them until they turn the check on). Deepgram gets only the recording's sound - not your name, handle, email, account id, city or location, and not even a link to where it's stored. every request opts out of Deepgram's model improvement program, so Deepgram **doesn't use the recording to train or improve its models**, and keeps it only while turning it into text.
- **what we keep:** the result (clear, not allowed or held), the moments and short quotes it flagged and which rule, and any topics it found (below). and **the text version**, kept privately on our server so a person on our team can review them if needed. it's never shown to other people. **it's deleted as soon as its walk or answer is deleted.**
- **people on our team** see held and reported items, their text, the flagged quotes and the poster's handle, and can listen to the recording. we're told about them by email, with the walk's name, the flagged quotes and private links to the recording (and, for a report, its photos) that stop working after 7 days. those emails stay in our own inbox.
- **we never identify anyone by their voice.** we don't make voiceprints or use voices to recognise people.

### sensitive topics

people talk about hard things on their walks - grief, illness, abuse, addiction, self-harm. we treat what you say about these as **sensitive personal information**, and use it only to run and protect the app:

- you can add topics and a "sensitive topics" heads-up to your walk, and the AI check can add them too (only we can take one the check added off). **the heads-up and topics are shown with your walk to the people who can see it**, so listeners know what's coming. anyone can choose to hide walks with sensitive topics.
- graphic details - like how someone hurt themselves, or anything that makes self-harm sound appealing - are held: you can cut that part, keep it for yourself, or ask for a person to look. we can't promise when.
- the AI check also notes if something sounds like **someone is in danger right now**. then the app privately shows them where they can talk to someone (call or text 988 in the US) and keeps their walk or answer just for them: it's never sent or shared, and nobody is told - not us, not anyone. (the one exception: if the same check also finds something sexual involving a child, it's removed and we're emailed.) this note is kept with the check, and only that person can see it in the app. we don't monitor walks and can't respond to emergencies. if you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number. to talk to someone now, call or text 988 in the US.

### your location

- **talkers:** while you record a talk-walk (including while it's paused), the app follows your location to draw the path you walk and place the spots you mark. if you allow it, this keeps working with the screen locked - only while that recording is going. your iPhone shows the location indicator whenever this happens. the whole path is uploaded with your walk and kept privately for you. your phone turns the walk's first point into its **city and country** (Apple does this for us).
- **what others can see of your path:** never its start or its end. you choose how much is cut off before it's shared - about 150, 400 or 800 meters, measured along the path - or hide your route completely. **spots on the part of the route that's shown appear on its map where you marked them**; spots in the hidden start and end, or on a walk whose route is hidden, are shown without a place. the city where your walk started is shown with it, including on anonymous walks.
- **listeners:** while you walk with someone, your phone uses your location to add up **how far you moved and for how long** (so a walk that barely moved stays just for you), and records **the path you walk** on your phone. at the start, your phone turns your location into **your city's name** (Apple does this for us); only the city is saved, and it's shown with your answers, including anonymous ones. your path is sent only if you choose to show your route with your answers, and then without its start and end.
- the app doesn't use your location when no walk is going.

### motion

while you record or listen, your phone counts **your steps** (Apple's motion & fitness), only on your phone, to help decide - together with GPS - whether you were really walking, so a walk counts even when GPS is patchy. **step counts are never sent to us or kept anywhere off your phone**, and nobody else ever sees them: we get only a yes or no, whether your phone says you walked enough. nothing else about your body or health is read.

### usage numbers

- the app notes, with your account, **the first time you reach each of a few steps** - like seeing the welcome screen, finishing the practice walk, starting or finishing a walk, passing the mic or posting - and when. never what you said, where you were or who you know. it stays in our own database (no outside analytics service), only we can see it, and it's deleted with your account.
- it also counts, with no account attached, how many times a few screens are opened each day (kept as daily totals), and how many phones open the app each day (under a random code made on your phone, deleted after 90 days).
- when the app hits an error, it sends us the error itself (where in the app, the message, the app's version and "iOS") under a random code made on your phone - never your account or anything you made. we delete these after 90 days.
- none of this is used for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

### notifications

if you turn notifications on, we keep your iPhone's notification token and your notification choices, so we can tell you when someone walks with you or answers you, when the talker hears your answers, when someone follows you or asks to, when someone you follow posts a walk, and when your walk or answer has been checked. a notification's record is deleted 7 days after it's sent. what shows in the app's bell is made from the activity itself, so it goes when that activity is deleted.

### reports and blocks

if you report a walk, an answer or a person, we keep what you reported, the reason, any details you add, the walk's name, and who reported it, so it can be dealt with. it's emailed to us. the person you reported is never told who reported them. we keep who you've blocked so the block works.

### keeping the app safe

to stop people guessing passwords, sending too many requests or overloading the app, the server counts recent requests by internet (IP) address, and for some actions by account, handle or email. each count is deleted automatically after 30 days. sign-up can use Cloudflare's check that you're not a bot, which sees your connection like any website does.

### the first version's features

can i walk with you? started as an app for walking routes. if you used that version, the routes, stops, photos and walks you made there, your follows, and your walk club answer (if you said yes, your email and handle are on the walk club list for news now and then; every walk club email has a one-tap unsubscribe link) stay in the same account, under this same policy, until you delete them or your account. searching for a place in that version sends what you typed and your rough location to Apple Maps or OpenStreetMap.

## what other people can see

- **your profile** can be seen by people signed in to the app, unless you've blocked each other. anyone who can open it sees your handle, photo, bio, Instagram username, how many walks you've posted and how many people walked them. your **display name** and **the city you set** are shown to everyone if your profile is public, and only to people you've approved if it's private. if you haven't set a city, the city of your latest walk they can see is shown. your profile photo is stored as a public file, so anyone with its link can open it.
- **a private profile:** people you haven't approved see your handle, photo, bio, Instagram username and those two counts - but not your display name, your walks, or the people you walked with. a walk you post for everyone from a private profile reaches only the people you've approved, unless you post it anonymously.
- **a walk for everyone**, once it's passed its check, can be heard by anyone signed in (except people you've blocked, and people who hide walks with sensitive topics, if yours has them) - with its name, description, topics, heads-up, spots, photos, city and (unless you hid it) its route without its start and end. **people who aren't signed in**, inside the app, can see a walk's card (its name, description, topics and heads-up, length, how many questions it asks, when it was posted, its city, and your name and photo unless it's anonymous) but can't hear it or see its cover, photos or route. "just me" walks, journal entries and walks saved for later are yours alone.
- **anonymous** walks and answers show "anonymous" - never your name, handle or photo - and never appear on your profile. their city still shows. we still know who posted them (see our terms).
- **answers:** "keep it for myself" - only you. "send it to [talker]" - only the talker. "share it publicly" - anyone who can hear that walk, under it; if it isn't anonymous, it also appears on your profile, for people who can see your profile. if your profile is private, a public answer is shown under your @handle (not your display name) to anyone who can hear the walk. answers saved for later reach nobody until you send them.
- **who walked a walk:** a walk shows how many people walked it (everyone who finished it, including people who kept their answers private - counted, never named). the list under it names only people who shared their answers publicly; the talker also sees who sent answers to them.
- **the top list:** walks that many people walked can appear in the app's "top" list (by week, month and all time, in the world, a country or a city), which people who aren't signed in can see too. a walk in the top 10 shows its place on the walk and on your profile.
- **the lock screen:** while someone listens to your walk, their iPhone's lock screen shows the walk's name, your name (or "anonymous"), its city and who's speaking - never what anyone said.
- **"share in other media":** only a walk or answer whose switch you turned on (it's off unless you do) may be featured by us in other media, like videos, books or films - with credit to your @handle, or as "anonymous" with no name. only answers shared publicly can be featured. your switch and its history are seen only by you and us.
- **blocking** someone means they can't see or hear your walks or answers, or send you answers, and you won't see theirs - anonymous ones included. they aren't told.
- **in lists, cards and notifications**, a private account appears by its @username, never its display name.
- **never shown to anyone else:** your age answer, drafts, saved walks, your journal, your check results (except the topics and heads-up shown with a walk or an answer), your settings, who you've blocked, and your reports.

## who helps us run the app

each service gets only what it needs to do its job for us, and works for us under its own terms and privacy protections.

- **Supabase** stores the app's data, recordings, photos and videos, handles sign-in, and runs the server code that does the check and sends emails and notifications. our data is kept in the United States (US West).
- **Anthropic** (Claude) checks the text of walks and answers before they're shared (see "the AI check").
- **Deepgram** turns a walk's or an answer's recording into text, only when the phone can't make the text itself or someone reports it (see "the AI check").
- **Resend** sends the app's emails (sign-up codes, password resets and email changes, and reports and held items to us).
- **Apple:** Sign in with Apple, notifications, speech recognition on your phone (the app doesn't send recordings to Apple for it), turning a location into a city name, step counting on your phone, maps, and the lock-screen card while you walk.
- **OpenStreetMap** for place search in the first version's route features, and **Cloudflare** for the "not a bot" check at sign-up when it's on.
- this website is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. it has no cookies, analytics or tracking.

we don't use any advertising, analytics or crash-reporting companies.

if the app or Hey Stranger LLC is ever sold or merged, your information may transfer to the new owner, who must keep following this privacy policy. we'll tell you before that happens.

## deleting things

when you delete a walk or an answer, it's deleted from the app right away.

- **a walk:** open it, tap "⋯" → delete. its recording, photos and videos are deleted for everyone. answers other people recorded to it are theirs: they stay, private to each listener, with your name as the talker (or "anonymous").
- **an answer:** open it, tap "⋯" → delete what i said.
- **a journal entry:** open your journal and delete that day.
- **your account:** your profile → settings → delete account, then type "delete".

**what deleting your account removes, straight away:** your account, profile and photo; your walks, answers and journal, with their recordings, voice versions, photos and videos; your drafts and saved walks; their text versions and checks; your follows, blocks, settings and notification token; your age answer; your walk club answer; the steps you reached; your "share in other media" history; and the record of the agreements you ticked. if you signed in with Apple, we ask Apple to disconnect the app from your Apple ID.

**what's kept:**

- **reports**, ones you made and ones about you, with what was reported, so we can still deal with them.
- **your handle**, held for 30 days so nobody else can take it straight away, then removed.
- **answers other people recorded to your walks** (above).
- request counts, until their 30 days are up.
- a list of the ids (only the ids) of deleted walks and answers, so a phone can never upload them again.

**copies that take a little longer to go:**

- a notification already delivered to someone's phone stays in their iPhone's notification list until they clear it - it can't be taken back. tapping it then says the walk is no longer there.
- a walk someone was in the middle of listening to may still be on their phone, but it won't play once the app can reach our server.
- our database provider keeps daily backups for recovery, for up to **7 days**. they hold database rows as they were that day (like a walk's name, the check's text and settings) - **never recordings, photos or videos**, which aren't in the backups. we don't restore a backup to bring back something you deleted.
- Anthropic deletes the text it checked within **30 days** (see "the AI check").
- the spots and passes of the mic saved as you record are deleted **2 days** after they're saved (by the next nightly clean-up), whether or not you deleted anything.

## how long we keep things

- **walks, answers, recordings, photos, videos, your journal, your profile:** until you delete them or your account.
- **a recording not yet posted or saved:** on your phone only.
- **parts cut while re-recording:** deleted at once.
- **text versions (for the check), and check results:** deleted as soon as the walk or answer is deleted.
- **daily database backups:** up to 7 days (database rows only, never recordings, photos or videos).
- **the text, at Anthropic:** up to 30 days (up to 2 years only if Anthropic's own safety systems flag it as breaking its usage policy).
- **a recording at Deepgram** (when the phone can't make the text, or it's reported): only while it's being turned into text.
- **spots and passes of the mic saved while you record:** 2 days after they're saved.
- **your age answer, agreements, settings:** until you delete your account.
- **the email link to a held recording:** stops working after 7 days.
- **sent notifications:** 7 days.
- **error reports and daily phone codes:** 90 days.
- **request counts:** 30 days.
- **a deleted account's handle:** held 30 days.
- **a sign-up whose email code is never typed:** 7 days.
- **reports:** kept, so they can be dealt with.

## paying

## people under 18

can i walk with you? is only for people 18 and over. the app checks your age at sign-up. we don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. if we learn someone under 18 has an account, we hide everything it posted and remove it. if you think someone under 18 is using the app, report them or write to hello@caniwalkwithyou.com.

## your privacy rights, including in California

wherever you live, you can ask us, at hello@caniwalkwithyou.com:

- to **know** what personal information we have about you, and get a copy;
- to **correct** it;
- to **delete** it (or delete your account yourself in the app);
- how we use it.

**California residents** have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA): to know what we collect, use and disclose (the categories are in "what we collect" above, the sources are you and your phone, and the purposes are running, protecting and improving the app); to delete; to correct; to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information - **we don't sell or share it** (for money or for cross-context advertising), and haven't in the past 12 months; to limit the use of sensitive personal information - **we use it only to provide the app**, as the law allows; and not to be treated differently for using these rights. you, or someone you authorise, can ask at hello@caniwalkwithyou.com; we'll check it's really you (usually by confirming from your account's email) and answer within 45 days. under California's "Shine the Light" law, you can ask whether we've shared your information with others for their own marketing - we haven't.

**outside the United States:** the app and its data are run from the United States. if you use it from somewhere else, your information is sent to and kept in the United States, and you have the rights above.

## security

your connection to the app is encrypted. access rules in the database mean people can only see and hear what you've allowed. talk-walk recordings and photos are only shared through links that stop working after 10 minutes, and only for files a walk or answer still uses. profile photos are public files, and so are the photos and videos of public routes from the first version; a private profile's first-version photos reach its approved followers through links. no system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe. if a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

## changes to this policy

if we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect. the date at the top shows when it last changed.
